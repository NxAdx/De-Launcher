De-Launcher · Design System v1.0

Focus.
Simplified.

A calm, intentional visual system for a focus-first Android launcher. Neutral surfaces carry the interface; sage green signals attention, progress and action.

Logo direction: abstract D + eye/leaf form, no internal “e”.

07 · UX principles

Do

Keep 90% of the UI neutral and let green identify intentional actions.

Do

Prefer fewer, larger decisions on the home screen over dense app grids.

Do

Use motion sparingly: 180–250ms, ease-out, no decorative animation.

Avoid

Bright wallpapers behind text and colorful icons competing for attention.

Avoid

Using green on every control; it destroys its meaning as a focus signal.

Avoid

Adding an “e” back into the mark unless it materially improves recognition.

08 · Product UI direction

ScreenRecommended direction
HomeQuiet wallpaper, large clock, one Daily Focus surface, essential apps first.
SearchSingle low-emphasis entry point; search everything without becoming visually dominant.
SettingsGrouped cards, concise descriptions, green selected states, progressive disclosure where possible.
Focus toolsMake “Auto-Arrange Non-Distractions” and Daily Focus feel like core product features, not secondary toggles.
